LeadHunter AI Desk - Quick Start Guide

Launch cold outreach campaigns in 4 simple steps.

1

Configure Your API Keys (Bring Your Own Key)

Go to Credentials & Settings. Enter your free or paid OpenRouter API Key (choose models like DeepSeek V3, Claude, or Llama) and your SMTP2GO API Key with your verified sender email.

2

Find or Add Target Prospects

Choose any of the 4 methods:

  • AI Web Search (Top Bar): Enter Target Niche, City, lead count, and click Discover.
  • + Add Prospects: Add a lead manually, paste raw listings via Smart Paste, or import any .CSV file.
3

Select Language & Draft AI Outreach

In AI Prompt Template, pick your target language (Spanish, English, French, German...). Check the prospects you want and click Draft AI. Each prospect receives a tailored HTML email.

4

Review & Send Campaign

Click Edit Email to view the real-time rendered HTML preview or tweak the source code. When satisfied, click Send Campaign to dispatch outreach safely via SMTP2GO with anti-spam pacing.
